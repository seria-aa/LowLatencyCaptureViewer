Low Latency Capture Viewer v1.2.0 x64 (continued from the v0.14.2 rework)

v1.2.0 settings, defaults, fullscreen cursor, and Exclusive preflight release
-------------------------------------------------
- Added a per-output-device WASAPI Exclusive event-path preflight. Each active
  endpoint is tested at 5-40 ms before Exclusive is offered, because some
  drivers accept initialization but deliver unusable event timing in practice.
- The settings dialog labels each endpoint as available with its first passing
  buffer duration, unavailable, or checking. Cached outcomes are reused across
  later settings openings and launches; the explicit button forces a rescan.
- Shared remains the default and ASIO remains available. The capture, D3D11
  latest-frame renderer, PCM queue, and normal WASAPI Shared paths are unchanged.


v1.1.3 final release
----------------------------------------------------
- Pixel-perfect fullscreen now keeps strict 1:1 output when the capture fits
  the monitor, centers it, and clears unused space to black. Only oversized
  captures are downscaled with their aspect ratio preserved.
- Relative window sizing now uses the same limiting-dimension definition when
  calculating and restoring the saved ratio. This prevents an extra shrink on
  16:10, 21:9, and 32:9 displays.
- Existing custom relative sizes are preserved; only an exact match for the
  legacy mixed-aspect formula is migrated once.
- F2 settings reopen on the saved viewer monitor and are explicitly activated.
  Moving the settings dialog no longer changes the viewer's sizing baseline.
- The capture, latest-frame-only D3D11, and WASAPI paths are unchanged and no
  new persistent queue is introduced.

v1.1.0 frame-rate and pixel-perfect display release
---------------------------------------------------
- Added selectable 30 fps capture. Exact 30/29.97 media types are recognized,
  and UVC drivers that expose 30 fps only through VIDEO_STREAM_CONFIG_CAPS are
  supported by configuring the capture pin itself at 30 fps.
- Pixel-perfect fullscreen now uses the largest integer scale that fits and
  clears unused space to black. Oversized captures retain their full picture
  with aspect-preserving downscale instead of cropping.
- Added F5 to restore an exact 1:1 client area without changing the saved
  Pixel-perfect option. A matching monitor uses borderless fullscreen; an
  oversized 1:1 request reports that it cannot fit.
- Matching capture and monitor resolutions again enter automatic borderless
  fullscreen, including non-pixel-perfect mode. A genuinely smaller saved
  monitor-relative window is still preserved.
- Fullscreen and F5 output changes rebuild only the D3D11 output resources.
  DirectShow capture and WASAPI continue running, so no persistent frame or
  audio queue is introduced.
- H.264/AVC and MPEG-4 were removed from compressed compatibility because
  inter-frame decode/reordering cannot provide reliable low latency. MJPEG is
  the sole experimental compressed mode and remains visible when all raw modes
  at the selected resolution are below 30 fps.

v1.0.6.2 Beta settings clarity and capture-audio preflight
----------------------------------------------------------
- Reorganized the startup settings dialog into an essential view and an
  expandable advanced view. Frequently used audio, display, window-behavior,
  language, and direct-start choices remain visible without expanding it.
- The selected video filter is inspected while settings are open to determine
  whether it exposes a built-in DirectShow audio output pin. When present,
  automatic capture audio is shown as built-in; otherwise the separate capture
  audio-device selector remains available.
- The preflight only enumerates pins and never runs a capture graph. It is not
  part of the video present loop, DirectShow callback, PCM ring, or WASAPI
  render thread, so it adds no steady-state capture latency or frame queue.
- When a selected device/resolution exposes NV12 or YUY2, compressed beta
  formats are hidden from the pixel-format selector to keep the tested raw
  low-latency path as the clear default.

v1.0.6.1 Beta compressed-capture compatibility and volume boost
---------------------------------------------------------------
- Added opt-in experimental MJPEG, H.264/AVC, and MPEG-4 capture modes. The
  DirectShow capture graph still retains only the newest compressed sample;
  Windows Media Foundation decodes it to NV12 for the existing D3D11 renderer.
- Compressed modes remain beta: supported behavior depends on the selected
  capture device's media type, sequence headers, and decoder availability.
  NV12/YUY2 remain the tested recommended path.
- Added an off-by-default setting permitting 5% mouse-wheel volume steps up to
  200%. Values above 100% apply in-app digital gain only and may clip loud
  source material; no audio buffer or frame queue is added.
- The startup settings dialog now shows a lower-left `v1.0.6.1 Beta` version
  watermark.

v1.0.6 direct startup and settings recovery
-----------------------------------------
- Added an off-by-default option to launch directly with saved settings,
  skipping the startup settings dialog.
- Hold Shift while launching to show settings for that launch, or press F2 in
  the viewer to stop capture safely and reopen settings.
- A capture initialization failure now returns to settings after its error
  message, preventing a direct-start configuration from becoming a dead end.

v1.0.5 window-state and capture-resolution behavior
----------------------------------------------------
- Fresh configurations start with 1920x1080 capture selected. The selected
  capture resolution is persisted and never changes merely because either
  window moves to a different monitor.
- Monitor-relative sizing restores its saved window-coverage ratio on launch.
  It now takes priority over automatic Pixel-perfect fullscreen, including when
  the selected capture resolution happens to match the restored monitor.
- Pixel-perfect startup fullscreen remains available when monitor-relative
  sizing is disabled. Esc closes that automatic fullscreen directly.

v1.0.4 display scaling and capture-ownership guidance
------------------------------------------------------
- Added Smooth and Sharp scaling choices for non-pixel-perfect video. Sharp
  uses the supported D3D11 Video Processor edge-enhancement filter and falls
  back to Smooth on drivers that do not expose it; no frame queue is added.
- Capture-initialization errors now advise closing OBS and vendor capture
  utilities that may already hold the selected device.

v1.0.3 separate capture-audio support

- GC573 keeps its existing single-filter video+audio path.

v0.15.9 D3D11 foreground-return recovery
-----------------------------------------
- A transient VideoProcessor/overlay/Present failure no longer immediately
  stops the single capture graph and closes the viewer.
- On failure only, log `GetDeviceRemovedReason` and rebuild the D3D11 device,
  upload surfaces, video processor, overlays, and swapchain in place.
- DirectShow capture and WASAPI remain running during up to three bounded
  renderer attempts (immediate, +100 ms, +250 ms).
- `DXGI_STATUS_OCCLUDED` is treated as a non-fatal hidden/covered state and
  tested every 50 ms until presentation is available again.
- The normal successful frame path adds no polling, sleep, device-reason query,
  queue, or frame. Existing HRESULT checking is reused, so latency is unchanged.

v0.15.8 volume and background-mute update
-----------------------------------------
- Mouse-wheel volume now changes in 5-percent steps and saved legacy values
  are rounded to the nearest 5 percent when loaded.
- Add a persistent `MuteWhenBackground` checkbox, disabled by default.
- Losing application activation ramps the render gain to zero; reactivation
  restores the saved volume. Capture, PCM queue consumption, and WASAPI stay
  running, so background muting does not accumulate latency or restart audio.
- The Tab OSD distinguishes background auto-mute, zero volume, attenuation,
  and the 100-percent PCM gain-loop bypass.
